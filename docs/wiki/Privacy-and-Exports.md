# Privacy review and exports

Open **Privacy review** after reading the findings. The page lists the categories and JSON paths changed before the case is saved or exported.

![Privacy review](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/loofiboss-bit/loofi-supportkit/main/docs/screenshots/privacy-review.png)

Automated redaction catches common identifiers and credential-like values, but unusual free text still needs human review.

## Choose an export

- **ZIP:** preferred support bundle with a static report, structured evidence, manifests, and checksums.
- **HTML:** offline, script-free report for reading.
- **JSON:** full versioned case for technical tools and long-term storage.

![Export choices](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/loofiboss-bit/loofi-supportkit/main/docs/screenshots/export.png)

Exports remain local until you share them yourself. Use a channel you trust and share only with the intended recipient.
